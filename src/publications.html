Through the Looking Glass: MIRA, a Mirror Reasoning Benchmark for Vision-Language Models First author

CVPR 2026 MMRAgI Workshop · Carnegie Mellon, Prof. Min Xu's Lab

Details

When a clock reads 2:50 in a mirror, humans instantly infer the true time is 9:10, yet vision-language models overwhelmingly report “2:50,” hallucinating the canonical orientation rather than reasoning about the reflection. MIRA is a benchmark built to diagnose this: 713 real-world images and 3,529 question-answer pairs spanning 10 scenario categories, from licence plates in rear-view mirrors to analog clocks, chemical formulas and ambulance mirror-text. The evaluation covers 11 models across more than 54,000 queries, and all of them achieve only 39–48% accuracy against 94% for human participants.

MVEB: Massive Video Embedding Benchmark Accepted

Adnan El Assadi, Roman Solomatin, Isaac Chung, Chenghao Xiao, Deep Shah, Manan Dey, Shriya Sudhakar, Zacharie Bugaud, Wissam Siblini, Ayush Sunil Munot, Yashwanth Devavarapu, Rakshitha Ireddi, Michelle Yang, Márton Kardos, Niklas Muennighoff, Kenneth Enevoldsen

EMNLP 2026 · arXiv:2606.14958

arXivCode & leaderboard

Details

A 23-task benchmark evaluating 33 video embedding models across classification, zero-shot classification, clustering, pair classification, retrieval and video question answering. No single model dominates, and performance is modality-dependent: audio's contribution turns out to hinge on dataset annotation provenance, helping when labels were produced from both modalities and hurting when they came from visuals alone, a six-point gap consistent across model families. Derived from a 184-task pool and integrated into the MTEB ecosystem.

EdgeMedBench: Benchmarking Small Language Models for Medical QA First author

IC4AI 2026

Details

An evaluation of five small language models across 45 configurations for medical question answering under edge deployment constraints, identifying an INT4 deployment that reaches 50% average accuracy within 1.18 GB of VRAM at 190 ms inference latency.

ML-Based SDN DDoS Detection: Machine Learning–Enabled Intrusion Detection System

CIMA 2026

Details

A machine-learning intrusion detection framework for software-defined networking, evaluating multiple classifiers on CICIDS-2017 to identify DDoS, DoS, brute-force, probe and user-to-root attacks. It uses features already available at the SDN controller, avoiding computationally expensive deep packet inspection, which keeps it light enough to integrate directly with the control plane.

Wavelet Diffusion for Unpaired 3D Brain MRI Generation In progress

In preparation · Harvard Medical School / Massachusetts General Hospital

Details

A wavelet diffusion model for unpaired 3D brain MRI generation, reducing reconstruction MSE from 0.088 to 0.0022 over more than three million training steps, with segmentation conditioning achieving above 0.92 SSIM. Current work extends to anatomically conditioned and longitudinal brain MRI prediction, implementing MSC label conditioning and consistent evaluation pipelines that assess anatomical fidelity and prediction quality across time points.

LLM Reliability in Decision Analysis In progress

In preparation · Stanford University, MS&E

Details

Decision analysis is an unusually clean domain in which to test autonomous LLM reasoning: the correct answer to every question is known exactly, in closed form, before any model is queried. The work uncovers a response-ordering artifact in which answer-first prompts produce values ranging from $60 to $82.35 while reasoning-first prompts consistently produce $76.00, demonstrating how evaluation design can distort conclusions about LLM reasoning. A code-generation evaluation pipeline reaches exact solutions on 128 of 128 decision problems where in-context reasoning fails, and error feedback improves GPT-4o from 10.2 to 23.0 out of 29 tests while leaving a performance gap.
